<section id="overview">
  <div class="container">
    <h2> Overview</h2>
    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>The Problem</h3>
        <p>Data centers consume ~2% of global electricity and millions of gallons of water daily, with significant carbon emissions from grid electricity.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Our Solution</h3>
        <p>AI-powered optimization that schedules workloads based on weather conditions and grid carbon intensity to minimize energy, water, and carbon.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>The Impact</h3>
        <p>10-25% energy savings, 10-25% water savings, 15-30% carbon reduction, with 100% SLA compliance.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
